Data scope & caveats

Coverage

122 applications with valid reviews across Banking (46), Payment (29), and Pindar (47). The analytical corpus contains 4,232,749 valid unique reviews over October 2024–September 2026. September 2026 is partial/provisional through 19 September.

Responsible interpretation

Reviews are self-selected and are not a probability sample. Prevalence estimates represent model-predicted review signals, not population incidence. Severity is a rule-based proxy, not independently human-validated monetary harm. Raw review text is not published here.
